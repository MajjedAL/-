Who is Doctor Doom?

Victor von Doom is one of Marvel's most recognizable villains in the comics: a brilliant inventor, a powerful ruler, and a master of both science and sorcery. His ambition and intelligence make him a threat who cannot be understood through strength alone.

As the MCU moves into stories involving new teams and different realities, learning about Doctor Doom's place in Marvel lore can add context to the road ahead. The details of his role in the film should be confirmed through official announcements rather than rumors.
